# Governance Metrics Framework

| Metric | Target | Current value | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Patch compliance | 95% or higher | 85% | Below target; investigate and remediate |
| Vulnerability remediation time | 7 days or less | 12 days | Above target; remediation is too slow |
| Security incidents | 0 | 1 | Incident recorded; investigate and document lessons |

## Reporting and accountability
- **Frequency:** Monthly, with urgent escalation for critical risks.
- **Owner:** Assigned security or risk-control owner.
- **Evidence:** Timestamped scan results, incident records, remediation tickets, and verification tests.
- **Escalation:** Report missed targets to management and track corrective actions to closure.
- **Validation:** Confirm metric definitions, calculation methods, and source data before executive reporting.

A metric status must reflect whether the target is met. Lower remediation time and fewer incidents are generally better; the direction of each metric matters.
